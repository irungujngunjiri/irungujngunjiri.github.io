About

I am Irungu J. Ngunjiri. I am a passionate youth leader and a student pursuing a Bachelor of Real Estate at the University of Nairobi.

I was born in a small village in the Rift Valley called Tandare, Laikipia County. I’ve grown up in Limuru, Kiambu County. I went to Chania Boys High School in Thika. Later I joined university to pursue a Bachelor of Real Estate at the University of Nairobi. Outside studies, I enjoy music, especially gospel.
